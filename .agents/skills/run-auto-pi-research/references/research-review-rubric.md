# Research Review Rubric

Use this rubric for novelty assessment and memo drafting.

## Novelty

- Direct duplicate: same exposure, outcome, geography, unit, data source, and method. Recommend `abort` or `reselect`.
- Partial overlap: same exposure/outcome family but different unit, source, method, time window, or execution angle. Usually acceptable with a clear contribution.
- Novel: no close empirical study found after targeted search. Proceed, but state uncertainty.
- Unavailable: search failed or returned too little evidence. Ask for explicit `proceed`, `reselect`, or `abort`.

## Feasibility

Prefer candidates with public, machine-readable exposure and outcome sources, tract/county join keys, no paid APIs, and low or medium automation risk.

## Memo Standards

State the research question precisely, avoid causal language unless the method supports it, cite every major literature claim, and make limitations concrete. The execution checklist should be actionable for a Python implementation pass.
